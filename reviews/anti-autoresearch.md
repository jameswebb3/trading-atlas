## Project overview

Research audit toolkit that connects numerical claims to source spans and deterministic checks. Produces traceable findings that help reviewers spot inconsistent tables, percentages, and reported arithmetic.

[Project documentation](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Anti-Autoresearch/blob/f3ed7577beaff9c44280f8fcddcfce0e51a6292d/README.md#L15-L28).

## Mechanism worth inspecting

The [README](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Anti-Autoresearch/blob/f3ed7577beaff9c44280f8fcddcfce0e51a6292d/README.md#L47-L53) describes a hashed evidence ledger and deterministic adjudication. The [numeric consistency checker](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Anti-Autoresearch/blob/f3ed7577beaff9c44280f8fcddcfce0e51a6292d/tools/check_numeric_consistency.py#L46-L95) validates percentages and deltas against operands while carrying claim ID, source span, file line, and hash. The [limitations](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Anti-Autoresearch/blob/f3ed7577beaff9c44280f8fcddcfce0e51a6292d/docs/limitations.md#L1-L42) reserve independent replication and substantive judgment for additional review.

## Important limit

A consistent equation can still use wrong inputs or conceal selection bias. Report which evidence was available and retain human review for methodological claims.

## Questions to take into your own design

- Can each published numerical claim point to immutable input spans?
- Does the review distinguish checked arithmetic from replicated results?

## Why this score for a trading system

3/5: Claim IDs, source spans, artifact hashes, and deterministic numeric checks form a concrete evidence pattern for research agents. The tool cannot establish that a reported experiment ran correctly or that its source data are valid.
