# Areas

Each review has one primary area, chosen by the main decision question. Topics that cut across areas appear as tags or in the review text. All ten areas appear in the index. The rated area pages contain only completed source reviews; the separate project ledger also groups unreviewed discoveries under provisional areas.

| Area | Questions covered |
| --- | --- |
| **Market Data & Ingestion** | Exchange feeds, on-chain indexing and canonicality, stream continuity, backfills, and cursors |
| **Data Quality & Lineage** | Contracts, completeness, point-in-time availability, transformations, and provenance |
| **Research & Reproducibility** | Experiment identity, preregistration, parameter capture, and repeatable results |
| **Signals & Models** | Signal mechanisms, feature construction, model assumptions, and failure conditions |
| **Backtesting & Simulation** | Event clocks, fill models, market mechanics, and replay/live comparability |
| **Execution & Exchange Integration** | Order intent, wire formats, acknowledgement, cancellation, fills, and reconciliation |
| **Portfolio & Risk** | Sizing, exposure, fees, limits, drawdown controls, and portfolio accounting |
| **Testing & Verification** | Property tests, adversarial traces, leakage checks, mutation, and independent oracles |
| **Operations & Observability** | Monitoring, alerts, restart recovery, incident evidence, and service reliability |
| **Agents & Decision Systems** | Agent evaluation, evidence capture, permissions, and decision governance |

A repository may support several areas. Its primary area reflects the question answered by that review, not every feature of the project. A second distinct question can justify a separate review of the same project later.
